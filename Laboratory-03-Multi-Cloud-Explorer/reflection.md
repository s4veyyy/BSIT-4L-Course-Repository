# Mission Reflection

## Multi-Cloud Explorer Reflection

Completing the Multi-Cloud Explorer mission gave me a better understanding of how the major cloud platforms differ and how cloud solutions should be selected based on business requirements. Among AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, **Google Cloud Platform impressed me the most** because of its strong capabilities in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. These technologies are important for modern applications and organizations that need to process large amounts of data or develop intelligent systems.

Although the three platforms have different strengths, **AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities**. All three provide cloud computing services such as virtual machines, object storage, networking, identity management, databases, and Kubernetes. They also use global infrastructures made up of geographic locations and provide tools that allow organizations to scale resources, improve availability, and manage cloud environments.

I learned that **business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection**. A startup may prioritize cost flexibility and scalability, while an organization using Microsoft technologies may benefit more from Azure. A company focused on artificial intelligence and analytics may prefer Google Cloud, while an organization needing a broad selection of cloud services may choose AWS. Therefore, choosing a cloud provider should be based on the organization's actual workload, existing technology, security requirements, budget, and future goals.

Acting as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** taught me that cloud engineering is not simply about knowing technical services. It also requires analyzing problems, comparing alternatives, understanding business needs, and explaining why a particular solution is appropriate. Creating service comparisons and client recommendations helped me practice making technical decisions based on requirements rather than popularity.

Finally, this mission improved my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** by adding organized research, comparison tables, client recommendations, screenshots, and technical documentation. The portfolio now demonstrates not only my knowledge of cloud platforms but also my ability to document technical work in a structured and professional way. The experience also helped me become more confident in using GitHub and Markdown while building a stronger foundation for future cloud computing activities.
